Auto-trader
Every analysis that ends in a BUY or SELL becomes a paper trade. The robot follows it on 5-minute candles: it waits for the entry, then records the stop, each target, or expiry. Results go into your journal automatically, after your broker's spread and commission (Settings). No real orders are sent.

Early-move signals: did they follow through?
Backtest the signals on history
Replays the robot's early-move detector over past candles, one closed candle at a time (no look-ahead), and checks each signal: did price go 1 ATR its way before 1 ATR against, and how would a simple trade have done (entry at the close, stop at the signal's stop idea, target 2R, at most 48 candles)? Results feed the signal record, so alerts can rely on hundreds of past signals instead of waiting months. Uses 2 Twelve Data credits (crypto: free).
Watchlist scanner
At each session open (Sydney, Tokyo, London, New York) Claude Haiku screens every pair on your watchlist and alerts you when it sees a possible trade. Between opens, the robot checks each closed 15-minute and 1-hour candle for the start of a move: a stop sweep and reversal, a change of character, a volatility squeeze breaking out, or a momentum candle. Strong signals are confirmed by Haiku before you are alerted, so you can run a full analysis early.

Alerts when the app is closed
A browser can't run while the app is closed. The background scanner runs on GitHub every hour, at no cost, and sends the same alerts to your Telegram. Set it up once:
  1. In Telegram, message @BotFather, send /newbot and copy the bot token. Then send any message to your new bot and open https://api.telegram.org/bot<token>/getUpdates to read your chat id.
  2. On GitHub, open this repository: Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions → New repository secret. Add ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, TWELVE_DATA_KEY, TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN and TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID.
  3. Create a fine-grained GitHub token for this repository only, with Contents: read and write. Paste it in Settings here, then tap Send watchlist to the background scanner.
